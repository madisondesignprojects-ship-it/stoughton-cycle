# Stoton Cycle website concept

A responsive, single-page redesign concept for Stoton Cycle in downtown Stoughton, Wisconsin. This repo is the source for the Vercel project `stoughton-cycle`.

## Research used
- Official site: https://www.stotoncycle.com/
- Stoughton Chamber listing: https://www.stoughtonwi.com/list/member/stoton-cycle-inc-22762
- Downtown Stoughton merchant directory: https://www.downtownstoughton.com/meetthemerchants
- Avid Cyclist directory (lists Phil Caravello as owner and says the shop has operated since 1995): https://avidcyclist.com/directory/stoton-cycle-inc/

## Business details in this concept
- Name: Stoton Cycle
- Address: 100 W Washington St, Stoughton, WI 53589
- Phone: (608) 877-1134
- Official website: https://www.stotoncycle.com/
- Published offering: bicycle sales and service, parts, accessories, clothing and helmets; road, mountain, hybrid, comfort, BMX and kids' bikes; e-bike sales and service.
- Brand mentioned on the existing site: Bianchi.
- Owner listed by Avid Cyclist: Phil Caravello. Verify directly before relying on this in a sales conversation.
- Hours shown here follow Downtown Stoughton's merchant listing: Tue 10 AM–6 PM, Wed 10 AM–6 PM, Thu 10 AM–7 PM, Fri 10 AM–6 PM, Sat 10 AM–4 PM, Sun and Mon closed. The shop's own website has Thursday at 10 AM–6 PM, so confirm the Thursday close before launch.

## Launch checks
- This is a private sales/demo concept, not an official shop website.
- Confirm current hours, contact details, owner information and any product/service copy with the business.
- Product imagery is illustrative and does not represent current in-stock items. Confirm image use rights and replace imagery with owner-approved assets before any production launch.
- Current inventory, pricing, warranties, availability and booking policies are not asserted.
- The original website remains unchanged.
